Wendong Guo, Yanwei Zheng, Junyi Wang, Mengbai Xiao, Xiao Zhang, Liang Ma, Dongxiao Yu

Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence 投稿稿Manuscript submitted to Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence

[PDF][SSRN]

研究概述Overview

Kangaroo 面向跨尺度、跨环境的连续导航。母车负责大范围运输，子车执行狭窄空间内的末端任务。迭代反馈规划器结合大语言模型、人员反馈、规则与场景先验分解需求；两车结合各自的定位建图、协同感知和路径规划完成对接与导航。稿件报告了仿真和真实场景评估。Kangaroo addresses continuous navigation across scales and environments. A macro vehicle handles long-range transport, while a micro vehicle carries out last-mile tasks in narrow spaces. An iterative feedback planner combines a large language model, human feedback, rules, and scene priors to decompose demands. Both vehicles use localization, collaborative perception, and path planning for docking and navigation. The manuscript reports simulated and real-world evaluations.

核心工作Key Contributions

  • 设计母车与子车的分工，使大范围运输和狭窄空间通行衔接。Defines a macro–micro division of labor linking long-range travel to access in narrow spaces.
  • 用需求驱动的反馈规划连接语言指令、环境规则与双车导航执行。Uses demand-driven feedback planning to connect language instructions, environmental rules, and coordinated navigation.
