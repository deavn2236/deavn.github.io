单目图像目标导航Monocular image-goal navigation

机器人根据当前 RGB 观察和目标图像预测行进路点，并在控制阶段考虑场景约束。A robot predicts waypoints from its current RGB view and a goal image, then accounts for scene constraints during control.

研究目标Research goal

图像目标导航希望机器人只依赖视觉观察，前往目标照片所对应的位置。难点在于图像之间缺少直接的几何位姿信息，行进路径还需符合环境与运动约束。Image-goal navigation asks a robot to reach a location represented by a target photograph using visual observations. The images do not directly specify geometry or pose, while the route must respect environmental and motion constraints.

方法与系统Method & system

PPT 概述了感知驱动的时空预测和约束感知的运动优化。对应的 SCTNav 稿件进一步描述 P3Net 如何融合过去、当前和目标视觉信息预测路点，以及 SCMPC 如何把场景约束接入运动控制。The slides describe perception-driven spatiotemporal prediction and constraint-aware motion optimization. The related SCTNav manuscript details how P3Net predicts waypoints from past, current, and goal views and how SCMPC brings scene constraints into motion control.

实机演示Real-robot demonstration

提供的演示包含室外和室内机器人行驶片段。该视频说明系统已在实机上运行；具体成功率与评估设置应以论文稿件为准。The supplied demonstrations show the robot moving in outdoor and indoor environments. The footage documents execution on a physical platform; success rates and evaluation protocols are reported in the manuscript.
