柔性与不规则物料抓取实验Flexible and irregular material manipulation

面向服装、食品等柔性与不规则物料操作，结合不同夹爪和机械臂开展抓取实验；多段实机演示展示夹持、搬移与处理过程。For handling clothing, food, and other deformable or irregular objects, experiments combine different grippers and robot arms; real clips demonstrate grasping, transfer, and material handling.

项目背景Background

柔性与不规则目标在形态、接触位置和可抓取区域上存在较大变化，需要通过实物测试观察夹持和搬移行为。Flexible and irregular objects vary in shape, contact, and graspable regions, motivating physical tests of holding and transfer.

与规则刚性物体相比，服装和食品物料更容易在接触后发生形变，抓取点选择和夹持方式会改变后续搬移行为。实物测试用于比较不同末端工具与材料的适配情况。Clothing and food materials deform on contact, so grasp location and holding method influence transfer behavior. Physical trials examine how different end-effectors interact with the materials.

任务说明Task

对工作台上的目标执行抓取、转移或材料处理动作，覆盖服装与食品物料等不同形态。Grasp, transfer, or process workbench objects across clothing and food-material settings.

在不同夹爪和机械臂配置下观察目标能否被夹持、提起和转移，记录柔性材料在操作中的形态变化；演示按服装抓取、食品物料与工作台处理分项呈现。Tests observe holding, lifting, and transfer under different gripper and arm configurations, including material deformation. Demonstrations are organized into clothing grasping, food materials, and workbench handling.

设备说明Robot hardware

实验配置包含不同机械臂、夹爪、工作台及食品处理装置。The slides show experiments using different arms, grippers, workbenches, and food-processing fixtures.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

现有材料主要记录机械臂动作和设备配置，可据视频分项展示夹持、搬移和处理过程。The available material documents robot actions and hardware configurations, supporting separate presentations of grasping, transfer, and processing.

技术重点在于目标形态、夹持接触与机械臂动作之间的匹配。实机记录按机械臂和末端工具配置展示夹持、提起、搬移及材料处理，可直接观察不同物料在动作执行中的形态变化。The technical focus is matching object shape, gripper contact, and arm motion. Physical records show holding, lifting, transfer, and processing under different arm and end-effector configurations, documenting material deformation during execution.

补充不同实验配置下的实机操作短片，展示物料抓取和搬移过程。Supplementary robot clips show material handling across the experimental configurations.
