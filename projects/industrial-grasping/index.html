柔性与不规则物料抓取实验Grasping flexible and irregular materials

通过机械臂实验观察服装和食品物料等不同形态目标的抓取与搬移。Robot-arm experiments with picking and moving clothing and irregular food materials.

实验背景Experiment context

与刚性零件相比，柔性和不规则物料的外形与接触状态变化更大。PPT 汇集了机械臂对服装及食品物料的操作片段。Flexible and irregular materials change shape and contact configuration more than rigid parts. The slides collect arm-operation clips involving clothing and food materials.

演示内容Visible experiments

视频可见机械臂对工作台上的目标执行抓取与搬移。现有材料未给出具体算法、实验协议或本人分工，故该页只描述可见的操作。The videos show a robot arm grasping and moving items from a workbench. The supplied materials do not specify an algorithm, evaluation protocol, or individual role, so this page describes the visible experiment only.
