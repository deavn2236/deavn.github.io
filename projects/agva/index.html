AGVA：2D 激光雷达与视觉融合导航AGVA: 2D LiDAR and vision fusion navigation

将平面激光扫描与相机点云结合，改进室内移动底盘对障碍物的感知与建图导航。Combines planar LiDAR scans with camera point clouds to broaden obstacle perception for indoor mapping and navigation.

问题与目标Problem & goal

单层 2D 激光扫描对不同高度障碍物的覆盖有限。项目引入相机深度信息，服务于室内移动底盘的障碍感知、建图和路径导航。A single 2D LiDAR plane has limited coverage of obstacles at different heights. The project adds camera depth information for obstacle perception, mapping, and indoor path navigation.

系统实现System design

激光数据与相机点云经过滤波、投影和融合，接入 Cartographer 建图和代价地图。PPT 还展示 RGB 深度与语义网络，以及语义代价扩展的探索；后者属于后续研究方向。LiDAR scans and camera point clouds pass through filtering, projection, and fusion, then feed Cartographer mapping and a navigation costmap. The slides also explore RGB depth and semantic networks and a semantic-cost extension as follow-up work.

演示Demonstration

实机视频展示平台在室内走廊与实验环境移动；配套材料包含地图和分割结果。现有视频主要展示导航环节。Real robot footage shows the platform moving through indoor corridors and lab spaces. Supporting slides show maps and segmentation outputs. The available footage chiefly demonstrates navigation.
