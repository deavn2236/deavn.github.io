果蔬干视觉分拣产线Vision-guided dried-fruit sorting line

识别定位待分拣物料，驱动四自由度机械臂抓取、放置并计数。Detects and localizes items for a four-degree-of-freedom arm to pick, place, and count.

生产任务Production task

产线中的果蔬干需要被识别、定位、抓取并放置到指定区域，同时记录数量。项目将视觉判断与机械臂动作接入既有机械硬件。Dried fruit on a pilot line must be recognized, localized, picked, placed, and counted. The project connects visual decisions and arm actions to the line hardware.

系统功能System functions

PPT 描述四自由度机械臂抓取、放置与计数，并与其他机械结构协同。我参与识别定位和机械臂操作环节。The slides describe four-degree-of-freedom arm pick-and-place and counting, coordinated with other mechanical components. My documented work involved visual localization and robot operation.

现场结果On-site outcome

PPT 记载预研产线通过验收，现场短片展示机械臂与产线。网页不据此推断量产部署、节拍或产能。The slides record acceptance of the pilot line, and on-site footage shows the arm and line. This does not imply full-scale production, throughput, or operating cadence.
