移动巡检操作Mobile inspection & interaction · 2023–2024

厂房自主巡检与设备按钮操作Autonomous plant inspection and button interaction

移动平台到达巡检位置，识别设备按钮，再由机械臂执行精确接触操作。A mobile platform approaches inspection points, recognizes device buttons, and uses an arm for precise contact interaction.

任务目标Task goal

设备巡检若需实际交互，机器人不仅要到达目标位置，还需判断按钮位置并控制机械臂接触。项目将移动导航与末端精确操作连成一个流程。Inspection that involves physical interaction requires more than reaching a location. The robot must locate a button and control contact with its arm. This project links mobile navigation to precise end-effector operation.

核心模块Core modules

PPT 描述传感器融合导航、视觉按钮识别和接触位姿反馈。我负责按钮识别与机械臂点击等关键环节，系统其余模块由项目团队共同开发。The slides describe sensor-fusion navigation, visual button recognition, and contact-pose feedback. My documented work focused on button recognition and arm clicking, within a team-developed system.

项目演示Project demo

提供的实机短片展示巡检平台。按钮点击的具体可见细节以视频镜头为准；网页不声称视频覆盖完整工业长期运行。The supplied real robot clip shows the inspection platform. Specific button-contact details depend on the visible footage; the demo does not establish long-term industrial operation.
